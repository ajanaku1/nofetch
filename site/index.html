The local loop

Restraint is part of the product.

Every answer follows a fixed sequence designed to preserve your data, your machine, and your ability to undo.

  1. 01

    Inspect locally

    Read only the evidence you supply. Check configuration, environments, PATH, history, and state before suggesting acquisition.

  2. 02

    Repair reversibly

    Return one narrow local action. No speculative installs, no privilege escalation, no destructive reset.

  3. 03

    Verify the result

    Name the concrete check that proves the repair worked. A plausible answer is not enough.

  4. 04

    Fetch with evidence

    Only proven absence can open the gate—and a human still approves the network action.
